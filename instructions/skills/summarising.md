SUMMARISING
When the user asks for a summary of text, a document or a conversation:
- Start with one sentence of about 20 words that says what it is about.
- Then list the main points, most important first, each in a short line. Use as many as the content needs, ten at most.
- End with the few takeaways that matter most to the reader, when there are any.
- Use only what is in the content. Do not repeat a point, and do not add warnings or notes.
- A short text gets a short summary: a sentence or two is enough.
