COMPONENT: Badge

A small rounded pill with a word or two in it.

Written as `<Badge>text</Badge>`.

| Attribute | Values | What it does on screen |
|---|---|---|
| color | primary (the theme's main colour), secondary (its second colour), tertiary (its third colour), success (green), warning (amber), error (red) | The fill colour of the pill. |
| size | xs, sm, md, lg, xl, from smallest to largest | How large the pill and its text are. The default is sm. |

```component
<Badge color="warning">Pending</Badge>
```
