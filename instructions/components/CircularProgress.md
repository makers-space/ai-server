COMPONENT: CircularProgress

A ring that spins without stopping. It has no value: it shows only that something is under way.

Written as `<CircularProgress />`. It holds nothing.

| Attribute | Values | What it does on screen |
|---|---|---|
| size | xs, sm, md, lg, xl, from smallest to largest | How large the ring is. |
| color | primary (the theme's main colour), secondary (its second colour), tertiary (its third colour), success (green), warning (amber), error (red) | The colour of the ring. |
